A weekly photo challenge with an octopus for a guide

Shoot more. Try new things.

ShuttInk gives you a reason to pick up your camera every week. Each week brings a new theme to shoot, a daily photo keeps the habit going, and a friendly community votes on the best shots. Ocho, a camera-obsessed octopus, keeps you company the whole way.

ShuttInk is in testing and coming soon to the App Store.

Why ShuttInk exists

Most photographers know the feeling: the camera sits on a shelf because there’s nothing in particular to shoot, or every outing ends with the same kind of picture you always take. Social feeds don’t help much either. They reward whatever already gets attention, so it’s easy to stop experimenting.

ShuttInk was built to fix that with three simple ingredients:

  • A prompt. A theme every week gives you something specific to go and find.
  • A rhythm. One theme a week and one daily photo turn “I should shoot more” into a habit.
  • An audience that cares. Other photographers see your work, comment on it and vote for it, and the best shots win.

It works for every level. A first-time phone photographer and someone shooting medium-format film get the same theme, and they’re judged on the same thing: the photo.

How a week works

  1. Monday: a new theme. Themes rotate automatically every Monday at midnight Pacific time, for example Portraits, then Landscapes, then Long Exposure. Each one comes with tips and suggested film stocks.
  2. All week: go shoot it. Interpret it however you like, on any camera. Tag your entry as Digital or Film, add a caption, and stamp it with the film you used.
  3. Submit one photo. One entry per theme keeps it about your best shot, not your most shots. Everyone who enters earns 50 shutters.
  4. Like what inspires you. Heart photos any time during the week. They’re saved to your profile so you can find them again later.
  5. The last day is voting day. Submissions close and everyone votes: one overall favorite plus one pick in each category.
  6. Results and a fresh start. Winners are announced, prizes are paid out, likes reset, and the next theme begins.

A photo a day

Alongside the weekly theme there’s the daily photo: one shot a day of anything at all. It’s a low-pressure way to keep your eye sharp between themes, and today’s photos from around the app show up in a strip at the top of the home feed.

Each daily photo earns 25 shutters, and every 5 days in a row adds a 50-shutter bonus. Your streak calendar shows the days you’ve shot, and if a streak breaks, Ocho lets you know so you can start the next one.

Voting and prizes

On voting day each person gets six votes: one for their overall favorite, and one each for Most Dramatic, Most Creative, Best Use of Light, Best Composition and Best Story. Categories mean a photo can win for what it does best, not just for being the most popular. You can’t vote for yourself.

Make Ocho yours

Every account comes with Ocho, your own octopus. Name him, pick his color, and spend your shutters in the Shop on hats, glasses and things for him to hold. He lives in a little room with a closet for everything you collect, and he reacts when you tap him (just don’t poke him too many times).

Clams are surprise boxes: open one to find sidekicks, rare outfits and animated profile banners. Sidekicks keep Ocho company. There’s Soupy the pug in his hoodie and shades, Merlin the black cat, who keeps dropping his fish and scooping it right back up, and plenty more. You can also customize your profile with frames and banners, and earn badges that can’t be bought, like a weekly win or a long streak.

A kind community

ShuttInk only works if people feel safe sharing their photos, so everyone agrees to our Terms of Use before they join. There is zero tolerance for bullying, hate, nudity, violence, spam or impersonation.

  • Report anything. Tap ••• on a photo or profile, or press and hold a comment. Ocho covers it for you right away while it’s reviewed, and you’ll get an update when the review is done.
  • Hidden when it matters. Content reported by several people is hidden for everyone until it’s been reviewed.
  • Block anyone. Blocking keeps someone’s photos, comments, notifications and friend requests away from you. They aren’t told.
  • Real consequences. Content that breaks the rules is removed, and so are accounts that keep breaking them.

Reports are private. The person you report never learns who sent it.

Our goals

  • Build the habit. Make shooting regularly feel easy and rewarding, so you grow simply by showing up.
  • Grow your range. Rotate through themes that stretch different skills, from light and composition to motion and storytelling.
  • Treat film and digital equally. Every photo competes on the same terms, whatever it was shot on.
  • Reward people, not algorithms. Photographers choose the winners, and category prizes recognize different strengths.
  • Respect your privacy. No ads, no trackers, and we never sell your information. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Keep it fun. An octopus with a camera is a reminder not to take any of it too seriously.
